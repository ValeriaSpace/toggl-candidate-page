04Is it a fit?

Freedom comes with fine print

Our culture works because the people here make it work. An honest look at both sides:

You'll thrive here if you…

  • Care about users and the quality of what you ship
  • Take ownership and follow through without being chased
  • Communicate clearly — and write things down
  • Speak up when something isn't working
  • Enjoy autonomy and can structure your own day

It might not be your place if you…

  • Are here mainly because "remote" sounds comfortable
  • Need meetings and supervision to keep momentum
  • Find written, async communication a chore
  • Want to skip team meetups — we expect everyone there

We'll be upfront: remote work and flexibility are part of Toggl. But if that's the only reason you're interested, this may not be the right match — and that's okay.

05Hiring process

Six steps, skills first

We look for two things: the right skills for the role, and Toggl's values. Steps vary by position and seniority — tap each step for details.

Our hiring is remote-first and async — on purpose.

Human interaction happens later in the process. Not because it matters less, but because our small Talent team is built to operate efficiently — it's how we guarantee every candidate a transparent and fair screening.
